Ruhao Zhang

Ruhao Zhang, male, was born in March 1995. He is a lecturer and master's supervisor in the Department of Information Resource Management, School of Sociology and History, Fujian Normal University. He completed an integrated master's–doctoral program at the School of Economics and Management, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences / National Science Library, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and earned a PhD in Management (Information Science).

His research spans scientometrics and informetrics, information visualization, and technological innovation analysis. He is especially interested in new applications of intelligent technologies in science and technology intelligence, including full-text bibliometrics and researcher mobility mining. He has published more than 20 papers in major Chinese and international journals, including Journal of the China Society for Scientific and Technical Information, Studies in Science of Science, Scientometrics, and Technological Forecasting and Social Change. He leads a project funded by the National Social Science Fund of China.

Experience

  • Lecturer and master's supervisor, Department of Information Resource Management, School of Sociology and History, Fujian Normal University, since August 2023. Courses taught include Informetrics, Informetrics Lab, Research Methods in Information Resource Management (lab component), and Information Analysis and Forecasting (lab component).
